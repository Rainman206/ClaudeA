Our Story

Born on
Cave Mountain

"There is a bear that lives behind our house on Cave Mountain."

Mountain King Brewing Company was born from a simple, extraordinary truth: there is a bear that lives on the mountain behind their home in Windham, NY. Their daughters named him the Mountain King — and so a brewery was born.

Nestled in the Catskills at the base of the ski slopes, Mountain King brews for the hikers, skiers, and mountain wanderers who make these peaks their playground. Every can tells a story. Every beer earns its name.

From the deep woods of Cocaine Bear to the golden fields of Sunshine Wheat, the King's domain is vast — and the beer is always worth the climb.
